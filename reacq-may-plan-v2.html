TVOD Strategy

₹20 Movie Pass · No mandate · 36hr access · Single title unlock

Approach

When a re-acq user lands on the paywall and rejects ₹99, instead of losing them, pitch "Don't want subscription? Buy just this movie for ₹20." Lower-friction transactional offer captures intent we'd otherwise lose. Bonus: TVOD purchasers become a paid-customer remarketing list — strong upsell candidates for ₹99 / tiered subscription later.

Why this matrix: Recency tells us memory state (Hot = remembers, Cold = needs new hook). Cycles tells us propensity (4+ = 3.1× conversion vs 1c). Combined, the matrix points each user to the right channel and content — and lets us prioritise spend on highest-yield tiers.
